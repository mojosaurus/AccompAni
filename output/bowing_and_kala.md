# Sarali Varisai — Bowing & Kala (Sanchara Bowing & Tri-Kala)

> Separate bows (⊓ / ∨), 2-note Jodi slurs, 4-note Anga slurs across bar lines, and 2nd-speed Dvitiya Kala.

## Practice Parameters

- **Raga:** Shankarabharanam — 29th Melakarta (Dheerasankarabharanam)
- **Scale (Bounded 1-Octave):** `S  R  G  M  P  D  N  Ṡ`
- **Tala:** Adi Tala (`4 + 2 + 2` = 8 Aksharas)
- **Total Exercises:** 7

---

## Bowing #01: Separate Bows (Prathama Kala)

- **Motif Swaras:** S R G M | P D | N Ṡ
- **Carnatic Classification:** Prathama Kala — Separate Bowing
- **Pattern Type:** 1 Full Bow Per Swara (⊓ / ∨)
- **Practice Focus:** Use full, even bow strokes (Down ⊓, Up ∨) from frog to tip with steady tone.

```text
S  R  G  M  | P  D  | N  Ṡ  ||
Ṡ  N  D  P  | M  G  | R  S  ||
```

---

## Bowing #02: 2-Note Slurs (Jodi Bowing)

- **Motif Swaras:** (S R) (G M) | (P D) | (N Ṡ)
- **Carnatic Classification:** Jodi Bowing — Paired Legato Slurs
- **Pattern Type:** 2 Swaras Per Bow Stroke
- **Practice Focus:** Connect each pair of swaras smoothly in one bow without breaking tone during finger drops.

```text
(S  R)  (G  M)  | (P  D)  | (N  Ṡ)  ||
(Ṡ  N)  (D  P)  | (M  G)  | (R  S)  ||
```

---

## Bowing #03: Cumulative 2-Note & 4-Note Slurs

- **Motif Swaras:** (S R) (G M) | (P M) | (G R)
- **Carnatic Classification:** Sanchara Slur Control
- **Pattern Type:** Mixed 2-Note & 4-Note Slurs Across Bars
- **Practice Focus:** Combines 2-note slurs with 4-note slurs spanning across tala bar lines (P D | N Ṡ).

```text
(S  R)  (G  M)  | (P  M)  | (G  R)  ||
(S  R  G  M)  | (P  D  | N  Ṡ)  ||
(Ṡ  N)  (D  P)  | (M  P)  | (D  N)  ||
(Ṡ  N  D  P)  | (M  G  | R  S)  ||
```

---

## Bowing #04: 4-Note Anga Slurs

- **Motif Swaras:** (S R G M) | (P D | N Ṡ)
- **Carnatic Classification:** Anga Bowing — Half-Cycle Slurs
- **Pattern Type:** 4 Swaras Per Bow Stroke
- **Practice Focus:** Divide bow speed evenly across 4 notes per stroke, including string crossing from M to P.

```text
(S  R  G  M)  | (P  D  | N  Ṡ)  ||
(Ṡ  N  D  P)  | (M  G  | R  S)  ||
```

---

## Bowing #05: 2nd Speed Separate Bows (Dvitiya Kala)

- **Motif Swaras:** SR GM PD NṠ | ṠN DP | MG RS
- **Carnatic Classification:** Dvitiya Kala — Separate Bows
- **Pattern Type:** 2 Swaras Per Akshara (Half-Bow Strokes)
- **Practice Focus:** Completes full Arohanam and Avarohanam in one 8-beat cycle using crisp middle-bow strokes.

```text
S R  G M  P D  N Ṡ  | Ṡ N  D P  | M G  R S  ||
S R  G M  P D  N Ṡ  | Ṡ N  D P  | M G  R S  ||
```

---

## Bowing #06: 2nd Speed 2-Note Slurs

- **Motif Swaras:** (S R) (G M) (P D) (N Ṡ)
- **Carnatic Classification:** Dvitiya Kala — Paired Slurs
- **Pattern Type:** Dvitiya Kala with Jodi Slurs
- **Practice Focus:** One bow stroke per akshara beat; builds fluidity for fast-tempo kriti passages.

```text
(S R)  (G M)  (P D)  (N Ṡ)  | (Ṡ N)  (D P)  | (M G)  (R S)  ||
(S R)  (G M)  (P D)  (N Ṡ)  | (Ṡ N)  (D P)  | (M G)  (R S)  ||
```

---

## Bowing #07: 2nd Speed 4-Note Slurs

- **Motif Swaras:** (S R G M) (P D N Ṡ)
- **Carnatic Classification:** Dvitiya Kala — 4-Swara Slurs
- **Pattern Type:** Dvitiya Kala with 4-Note Slurs
- **Practice Focus:** Plays the entire Arohanam in 2 bows and Avarohanam in 2 bows.

```text
(S R G M)  (P D N Ṡ)  | (Ṡ N D P)  | (M G R S)  ||
(S R G M)  (P D N Ṡ)  | (Ṡ N D P)  | (M G R S)  ||
```
