# Sarali Varisai — Bounded Motifs (Alankara Varisai)

> Stepwise and vakra 4-note motifs shifting strictly within S to Ṡ with zero octave wrapping.

## Practice Parameters

- **Raga:** Shankarabharanam — 29th Melakarta (Dheerasankarabharanam)
- **Scale (Bounded 1-Octave):** `S  R  G  M  P  D  N  Ṡ`
- **Tala:** Adi Tala (`4 + 2 + 2` = 8 Aksharas)
- **Total Exercises:** 3

---

## Alankara #01: S – R – S – R

- **Motif Swaras:** S – R – S – R
- **Carnatic Classification:** Alankara Varisai (Bounded Stepwise)
- **Pattern Type:** Adjacent Neighbor Oscillation (U–D–U)
- **Practice Focus:** Rapid 1st-finger alternation without tension; strengthens left-hand agility.

```text
S  R  S  R  | R  G  | R  G  ||
G  M  G  M  | M  P  | M  P  ||
P  D  P  D  | D  N  | D  N  ||
N  Ṡ  N  Ṡ  | N  Ṡ  | N  Ṡ  ||
Ṡ  N  Ṡ  N  | N  D  | N  D  ||
D  P  D  P  | P  M  | P  M  ||
M  G  M  G  | G  R  | G  R  ||
R  S  R  S  | R  S  | R  S  ||
```

---

## Alankara #02: S – R – G – R

- **Motif Swaras:** S – R – G – R
- **Carnatic Classification:** Alankara Varisai (Bounded Stepwise)
- **Pattern Type:** Step-Up & Turnaround Alankara (U–U–D)
- **Practice Focus:** Classic Alankara reversal motif; builds clean finger lift and bow-change control.

```text
S  R  G  R  | R  G  | M  G  ||
G  M  P  M  | M  P  | D  P  ||
P  D  N  D  | D  N  | Ṡ  N  ||
Ṡ  N  D  N  | N  D  | P  D  ||
D  P  M  P  | P  M  | G  M  ||
M  G  R  G  | G  R  | S  R  ||
```

---

## Alankara #03: S – R – G – M

- **Motif Swaras:** S – R – G – M
- **Carnatic Classification:** Alankara Varisai (Bounded Stepwise)
- **Pattern Type:** Stepwise Linear Ascent & Descent (U–U–U)
- **Practice Focus:** Even bow distribution across all 4 fingers on the Sa and Pa strings within one octave.

```text
S  R  G  M  | R  G  | M  P  ||
G  M  P  D  | M  P  | D  N  ||
P  D  N  Ṡ  | P  D  | N  Ṡ  ||
Ṡ  N  D  P  | N  D  | P  M  ||
D  P  M  G  | P  M  | G  R  ||
M  G  R  S  | M  G  | R  S  ||
```
