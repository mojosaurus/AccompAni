# Sarali Varisai — Bounded Thirds (Entry-Level Dhatu Varisai)

> Controlled single-skip intervals (leap ≤ 2) strictly bounded between S and Ṡ.

## Practice Parameters

- **Raga:** Shankarabharanam — 29th Melakarta (Dheerasankarabharanam)
- **Scale (Bounded 1-Octave):** `S  R  G  M  P  D  N  Ṡ`
- **Tala:** Adi Tala (`4 + 2 + 2` = 8 Aksharas)
- **Total Exercises:** 20

---

## Bounded Third #01: S – R – S – G

- **Motif Swaras:** S – R – S – G
- **Carnatic Classification:** Entry-Level Dhatu Varisai (Leap ≤ 2)
- **Pattern Type:** Alternating Dhatu Zig-Zag (Up–Down–Up)
- **Practice Focus:** Interleaves ascending thirds and steps across adjacent strings; builds bow-crossing agility.

```text
S  R  S  G  | R  G  | R  M  ||
G  M  G  P  | M  P  | M  D  ||
P  D  P  N  | D  N  | D  Ṡ  ||
Ṡ  N  Ṡ  D  | N  D  | N  P  ||
D  P  D  M  | P  M  | P  G  ||
M  G  M  R  | G  R  | G  S  ||
```

## Bounded Third #02: S – R – G – S

- **Motif Swaras:** S – R – G – S
- **Carnatic Classification:** Entry-Level Dhatu Varisai (Leap ≤ 2)
- **Pattern Type:** Rise-and-Turn Vakra Third (Up–Up–Down)
- **Practice Focus:** Climbs with a third skip and resolves downward; sharpens pitch memory on downward finger drops.

```text
S  R  G  S  | R  G  | M  R  ||
G  M  P  G  | M  P  | D  M  ||
P  D  N  P  | D  N  | Ṡ  D  ||
Ṡ  N  D  Ṡ  | N  D  | P  N  ||
D  P  M  D  | P  M  | G  P  ||
M  G  R  M  | G  R  | S  G  ||
```
