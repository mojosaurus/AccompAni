# Sarali Varisai — Cumulative Ceilings (Sarali Sthayi Extensions)

> Progressive range expansions (S→M, S→P, S→D, S→N, S→Ṡ) anchored back to Shadjam.

## Practice Parameters

- **Raga:** Shankarabharanam — 29th Melakarta (Dheerasankarabharanam)
- **Scale (Bounded 1-Octave):** `S  R  G  M  P  D  N  Ṡ`
- **Tala:** Adi Tala (`4 + 2 + 2` = 8 Aksharas)
- **Total Exercises:** 6

---

## Ceiling #01: Madhyama Frame (S → M → S)

- **Motif Swaras:** S – R – G – M
- **Carnatic Classification:** Sarali Sthayi Extensions
- **Pattern Type:** Sa-String Four-Finger Frame
- **Practice Focus:** Establishes pure intonation across S, R, G, and M before crossing to the open Pa string.

```text
S  R  G  M  | G  R  | S  ,  ||
S  R  G  M  | M  G  | R  S  ||
```

---

## Ceiling #02: Panchama Anchor (S → P → S)

- **Motif Swaras:** S – R – G – M – P
- **Carnatic Classification:** Sarali Sthayi Extensions
- **Pattern Type:** Open Pa-String Crossing
- **Practice Focus:** Trains clean bow crossing between M (3rd finger on Sa string) and open P with sustained karvais.

```text
S  R  G  M  | P  ,  | P  ,  ||
S  R  G  M  | P  M  | G  R  ||
P  M  G  R  | S  R  | G  M  ||
P  M  G  R  | S  ,  | S  ,  ||
```

---

## Ceiling #03: Dhaivata Extension (S → D → S)

- **Motif Swaras:** S – R – G – M – P – D
- **Carnatic Classification:** Sarali Sthayi Extensions
- **Pattern Type:** 1st Finger on Pa String
- **Practice Focus:** Introduces D (1st finger on Pa string) while anchoring returns to lower Shadjam.

```text
S  R  G  M  | P  D  | P  M  ||
G  R  S  R  | G  M  | P  D  ||
D  P  M  G  | R  S  | R  G  ||
D  P  M  G  | R  ,  | S  ,  ||
```

---

## Ceiling #04: Nishada Extension (S → N → S)

- **Motif Swaras:** S – R – G – M – P – D – N
- **Carnatic Classification:** Sarali Sthayi Extensions
- **Pattern Type:** 2nd Finger on Pa String
- **Practice Focus:** Adds N (2nd finger on Pa string) and checks tuning against open Tara Shadjam.

```text
S  R  G  M  | P  D  | N  D  ||
P  M  G  R  | S  R  | G  M  ||
S  R  G  M  | P  D  | N  D  ||
N  D  P  M  | G  R  | S  ,  ||
```

---

## Ceiling #05: Full Cumulative Climb (Sarali Rows 9–14)

- **Motif Swaras:** S → P → D → N → Ṡ → S
- **Carnatic Classification:** Sarali Sthayi Extensions
- **Pattern Type:** Classic Progressive Octave Climb
- **Practice Focus:** Traditional Sarali Varisai progressive ceiling climb from P up to Ṡ and full Avarohanam.

```text
S  R  G  M  | P  ,  | P  ,  ||
S  R  G  M  | P  D  | P  M  ||
S  R  G  M  | P  D  | N  D  ||
S  R  G  M  | P  D  | N  Ṡ  ||
Ṡ  N  D  P  | M  G  | R  S  ||
```

---

## Ceiling #06: Descending Tara Anchor (Ṡ → M → G → R → S)

- **Motif Swaras:** Ṡ → M → G → R → S
- **Carnatic Classification:** Sarali Sthayi Extensions
- **Pattern Type:** Upper-Octave Intonation Check
- **Practice Focus:** Reverses the cumulative drill from open Tara Ṡ downward to reinforce descending pitch memory.

```text
Ṡ  N  D  P  | M  ,  | M  ,  ||
Ṡ  N  D  P  | M  G  | M  P  ||
Ṡ  N  D  P  | M  G  | R  G  ||
Ṡ  N  D  P  | M  G  | R  S  ||
S  R  G  M  | P  D  | N  Ṡ  ||
```
